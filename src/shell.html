The zaibatsu were dissolved in 1945-47 and reassembled as keiretsu — cross-shareholdings, a presidents' council, a main bank and a general trading house. India's business houses are structurally closer to this model than to anything else in the ledger. Toyota Industries' 2026 buyout is now unwinding the cross-holdings for a ¥4.43 trillion book gain, just as the model becomes most instructive.
